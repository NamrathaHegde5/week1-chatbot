Apple Inc. reported total net sales of $383.29 billion in 2023, compared to $394.33 billion in 2022.

Products net sales were $298.09 billion in 2023.

Services net sales were $85.20 billion in 2023.

The decrease in net sales during 2023 compared to 2022 was primarily due to lower demand in certain product categories and foreign exchange impacts.

During 2023, the Company's net sales through its direct and indirect distribution channels accounted for 39% and 61%, respectively, of total net sales.
